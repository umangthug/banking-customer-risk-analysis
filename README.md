<h1 align="center">Banking Customer Risk & AML Analysis</h1>

<p align="center">
SQL + Tableau project focused on AML compliance, sanctions exposure, and customer risk analytics.
</p>

---

## Project Overview

This project analyses banking customer and AML compliance data to identify:

<ul>
<li>High-risk customers</li>
<li>Sanctions exposure</li>
<li>Politically Exposed Persons (PEPs)</li>
<li>FATF high-risk jurisdictions</li>
<li>Sector-based risk concentration</li>
<li>Data quality and compliance monitoring concerns</li>
</ul>

---

## Business Objective

Financial institutions must continuously monitor customer risk exposure to maintain regulatory compliance and reduce financial crime risks.

This project focuses on:

<ul>
<li>AML risk analysis</li>
<li>Sanctions monitoring</li>
<li>Customer risk segmentation</li>
<li>Compliance reporting</li>
<li>Operational risk visibility</li>
</ul>

## Key Business Insights

<ul>
<li>High-risk sectors showed elevated compliance exposure</li>
<li>PEP and sanctions-linked clients require enhanced due diligence</li>
<li>FATF and OFAC flagged jurisdictions contribute to increased customer risk</li>
<li>Data quality gaps can negatively impact compliance effectiveness</li>
</ul>
