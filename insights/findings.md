# Key Findings

## High Risk Exposure
A significant number of clients were associated with high-risk sectors and jurisdictions flagged under FATF and OFAC monitoring.

## PEP Analysis
The dataset identified politically exposed persons (PEPs), which require enhanced due diligence and monitoring.

## Sanctions Monitoring
Several clients showed sanctions-related indicators, highlighting the importance of ongoing compliance screening.

## Sector Risk Trends
Higher concentration of risk was observed in specific business sectors with elevated risk ratings.

## Data Quality Importance
Missing or inconsistent customer information can negatively impact risk assessment and regulatory compliance processes.
